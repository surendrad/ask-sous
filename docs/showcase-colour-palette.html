Ask Sous — Colour Palette Directions

Three complete directions, each with a dark and light mode. All are AA-contrast checked for text-on-background pairs. Toggle each option between dark/light, then pick the one that feels right — or tell me "A but with B's brand colour," etc.

Design philosophy: grounded not decorative · calm data density · fast feels honest.

Option A — Verified Teal

Cool, precise, trustworthy — the "your numbers are correct" feeling. Closest to a fintech/data-tool aesthetic (Linear, Vercel-adjacent).

Option B — Warm Ember

Terracotta/amber brand tone — ties back to hospitality and warmth without tipping into "generic restaurant red." Distinctive in an interview setting.

Option C — Signal Violet

Indigo/violet brand — the familiar "modern AI product" palette. Safe, polished, a little less distinctive than A or B.
